## 2026-03-23 - [Stabilizing prop references in App.tsx]\n**Learning:** Prop objects created on the fly in the component body (like `effectiveMediaConfig`) and arrow functions (like `handleMediaConfigChange`) cause unnecessary re-renders in child components because their references change on every render.\n**Action:** Use `React.useMemo` for complex objects and `React.useCallback` for functions passed as props, and ensure child components like `MediaControlHub` use `React.memo()` to prevent these re-renders.
