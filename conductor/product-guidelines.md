# Product Guidelines

## AI Co-Host Personality
- **Tone:** Enthusiastic and energetic. The assistant should be a high-energy "hype man" that uses modern slang and provides unwavering support to the streamer.
- **Switchable Personas:** The AI should support different personas that can be switched based on the stream's needs:
    - **Immersion Mode:** Stays strictly in character, reacting emotionally and authentically to game events.
    - **Utility Mode:** Focuses on precise translation and clear, professional narration of in-game text.
    - **Humor Mode:** Provides comedic commentary and intentional "over-the-top" voice acting for entertainment.

## Visual and Interface Guidelines
- **UI Framework:** Custom CSS powered by Tailwind.
- **Design Principles:**
    - **Vibrant Gradients:** Use energetic color transitions to highlight interactive elements.
    - **Rounded Elements:** Favor rounded corners for all containers and buttons to maintain a playful, friendly feel.
    - **Dynamic Visuals:** Incorporate subtle animations and transitions to reflect the AI's "energy."

## Content and Translation Guidelines
- **Retro Translation:** Ensure translations capture the spirit and quirkiness of classic games while remaining readable.
- **Voice Acting:** Maintain high quality even in "Humor Mode," ensuring the voice remains clear and the performance is intentional.
